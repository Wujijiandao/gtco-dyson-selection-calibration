# v1.1.0-rasti-transfer - 2026-09-21

This update follows the MNRAS decision on manuscript MN-26-2504-P. MNRAS declined the paper for scope and recommended transfer to RAS Techniques and Instruments (RASTI), noting that the manuscript is primarily methodological/technical rather than a paper reporting new astronomical results.

Changes in this working authority:

- retitled the manuscript to foreground selection-function calibration;
- rewrote the abstract around the auditable multistage response-calibration workflow;
- reframed the contribution paragraph as a methodological contribution;
- renamed the population-inference section to avoid implying a completed occurrence-rate result;
- added a transferability subsection for multistage rare-object searches;
- added the mandatory RASTI keyword `Data Methods`;
- retained and verified figure alt text for all five main-text figures;
- added separate Funding and Conflict of Interest statements;
- updated Data availability and bug-reporting information;
- added ScholarOne-compatible `\pdfminorversion=5`;
- added RASTI submission/transfer documentation and MNRAS scope-decision provenance.

Unchanged:

- numerical analyses and frozen outputs;
- figures and data products;
- the 0.910/0.414/0.402/0.878 headline values and W4 PSF-sensitivity results;
- scientific claim boundaries;
- the historical `v1.0.0-submission` tag and Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21896997.
